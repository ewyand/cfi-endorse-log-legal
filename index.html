Privacy Policy — CFI Endorse Log

Last updated: September 14, 2026

CFI Endorse Log is an iPhone app that helps flight instructors keep track of student endorsements. This page explains what information the app handles and how.

We don't collect your data

CFI Endorse Log has no user accounts, no login, and we do not operate any server that receives, stores, or has access to your data. Nothing you enter in the app — student names, certificate numbers, endorsement records, proof photos — is ever sent to us. The only data leaving your device is described below under Advertising, and it never includes anything you've entered into the app.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter — student names and contact info, certificate numbers, endorsement records, dates, and proof photos — is stored only on your own device, using Apple's on-device SwiftData storage. It stays there until you delete it yourself or delete the app.

Camera and Photo Library access

The app asks for camera and/or photo library access only so you can attach a proof photo to an endorsement record. Photos you capture or select this way are processed and stored locally on your device, the same as everything else — never uploaded anywhere by the app.

Network requests the app makes

The app makes two kinds of outbound network requests, neither of which includes any of your personal or student data:

  • Content updates: a background check against a public, read-only file that lets us ship corrected or newer FAA endorsement text without requiring an app update.
  • FAA edition check: a request to faa.gov to see whether a newer edition of the underlying FAA advisory circular has been published.

Both requests only download information — they never transmit anything about you, your students, or your device.

Advertising

The free version of CFI Endorse Log shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob ads are personalized based on your device's advertising identifier and activity, which requires your permission under Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework — you'll see a one-time system prompt asking to allow or deny this the first time a free-tier ad would show. If you decline, AdMob still shows ads, just less targeted to you. Google, as the ad provider, may collect device and usage data as described in Google's own privacy policy; we don't receive or have access to that data ourselves. Upgrading to Pro removes ads (and the AdMob SDK's activity) entirely.

Notifications

Any reminders the app schedules (for example, a certificate expiration reminder) are local notifications set up entirely on your device. No push-notification service or third party is involved.

Exporting or backing up your data

The app can create a backup export file at your request. Sharing that file — by email, saving to Files, AirDrop, or any other method — is entirely your choice, using Apple's standard share sheet. We never receive a copy.

Children's privacy

CFI Endorse Log is intended for use by certificated flight instructors and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted here with a revised "Last updated" date.
