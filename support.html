Support — CFI Endorse Log

CFI Endorse Log is an iPhone app for flight instructors to keep their own record of endorsements issued to students.

Contact

Questions, bug reports, or feature requests can be sent to info@cfiendorselog.com. We aim to respond within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?
Entirely on your own device — there's no account, login, or server. See the Privacy Policy for details.

What does the free version include?
The free tier lets you log endorsements for one active student, with the full 96-entry AC 61-65 endorsement catalog, proof photos, and backup export — the same features as Pro, just capped at one active student and with ads shown. Archiving a student frees up the slot for a new one.

What does upgrading to Pro do?
Removes the active-student cap and removes ads. It's a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

I lost my data after reinstalling — can I get it back?
Only if you made a backup export first (Dashboard menu → Export Backup) and still have that file. Since everything is stored on-device only, uninstalling the app or losing the device without a backup means the data can't be recovered.

Can I use this on an iPad?
Not in the current version — CFI Endorse Log is iPhone-only for now.
